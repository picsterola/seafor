§ D · Questions donors actually ask

The blunt FAQ.

Is my contribution disclosed publicly? +

Contributions to a Washington 501(c)(4) are not publicly disclosed at the donor level. PAC and independent expenditure activity, when it occurs, follows separate disclosure rules under WA PDC and SEEC. We'll walk you through which lane fits your goals before any commitment.

Is this a campaign committee? +

No. SeaFor is the 501(c)(4) civic infrastructure entity. Electoral activity runs through a separate PAC / IE structure with a clean firewall, designed under counsel review. The c4 builds the operating system. The PAC, when it operates, plays in its own clearly-marked lane.

What is the first 90-day budget? +

Counsel and legal setup, founding governance, the first public product (the City Hall tracker), partner alignment, and the 2026 District 5 test. We'll share the line-item budget on a 30-minute call. We don't publish it because it changes weekly as counsel sharpens the structure.

How small can a meaningful contribution be? +

The founding circle is small by design. If that's not the right fit, there are real ways to support that aren't financial: warm intros, civic partnership, candidate referrals, and content collaboration. Tell us what you've got. We're more interested in the right people than the largest checks.

Can I support privately while staying out of the politics? +

Yes. That's specifically what the 501(c)(4) structure was built for. Many of our supporters operate businesses, sit on boards, or hold positions where public political activity isn't tenable. The entity protects that.

What if I disagree with you on a specific issue? +

Good. SeaFor isn't an ideological project. We back outcomes-focused governance on public safety, homelessness, housing, permitting, and land use. If you and we agree on the frame, individual issue disagreements are features, not bugs.
